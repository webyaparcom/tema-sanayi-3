Reader questions

Questions when choosing a gearbox

Use «All» to open every answer at once. Figures, test reports and certificates are fictional (DEMO).

0 / 6

01What do you need to size a gearbox?

Motor power and speed, output speed or torque, daily running hours, shock load and mounting position. A photo of the label is also a good start.

02What is the service factor?

It is the ratio of the gearbox’s rated torque to the real load of the application. For shock loads and long running hours we suggest 1.5 or more.

03How often should the oil be changed?

With synthetic oil, at about 10,000 running hours; earlier if the oil often runs above 80 °C.

04Do you overhaul other brands’ gearboxes?

Yes; after dismantling and measuring, we quote separately for gears, bearings and seals.

05What is the lead time?

HE and KH series from stock in 3–5 business days; custom ratios and planetary units in 4–6 weeks (fictional).

06Where can I see the test report?

The report number is on the gearbox label; send it by e-mail and the report is returned as a PDF. Reports on this site are DEMO.
